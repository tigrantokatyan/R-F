The house code

Four principles
behind every piece

Small studio, long habits. This is what we hold to, from the first pattern block to the box on your doorstep.

01

Comfort is the first fitting

If it cannot be worn from a morning meeting to a late dinner, it does not leave the studio. Every block is tested on real bodies before it becomes a size run.

02

Small runs, no leftovers

We cut in limited numbers and restock what earns it. Nothing is made to sit in a warehouse, and nothing is discounted into meaninglessness.

03

One family, one standard

The people who answer your message are the people who packed your parcel. Since 2022 the whole thing has stayed in the family, on purpose.

04

Colour you can keep wearing

Shades are chosen to live together across seasons — so a piece bought this year still belongs beside the next one.

Good to know

The practical
details

Everything you would ask us at the counter.

We ship worldwide to 45 countries. Orders leave the studio within two working days; delivery takes three to seven days depending on the destination.

Every piece is cut from one pattern block, so your size carries across shades and styles. Between sizes, size down for a clean fit and up for the oversized look.

Combed cotton, 240 gsm on jersey and 320 gsm on outerwear. Wash cold, dry flat, and press on the reverse to keep the print and the colour.

Thirty days from delivery, unworn and with the tags on. Exchanges within the same shade ship free; refunds return to the original payment method.
